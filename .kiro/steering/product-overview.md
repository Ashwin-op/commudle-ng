# Product Overview

## Project Purpose

Commudle is a comprehensive community management platform designed specifically for tech communities worldwide. The platform streamlines the management, engagement, and growth of technical communities.

**Website**: [www.commudle.com](https://commudle.com)

## Value Proposition

Commudle provides a centralized solution for tech community organizers to:

- Manage community events, hackathons, and activities
- Facilitate member engagement and communication
- Track community growth and analytics
- Enable seamless event registration and participation
- Build and showcase community projects

## Key Features

### Community Management

- Community creation and administration with customizable profiles
- Community groups, channels, and member management
- Community badges and achievements

### Event Management

- Event creation, scheduling, and multi-track support
- Multiple registration types (simple, data form-based, paid)
- Event entry passes, locations, and collaborations
- Event updates, sponsors, and attendee communications

### Hackathon Platform

- Complete hackathon lifecycle management
- Team formation and collaboration tools
- Round-based submissions with scoring and judging
- Track management and sponsor visibility

### Content & Communication

- Discussion forums, personal chat, and posts/feed
- Newsletters, polls, surveys, and Q&A system
- Announcements and real-time updates

### User Features

- Comprehensive user profiles with work history
- User badges and engagement tracking
- Google Account and email-based authentication
- Role-based access control

### Forms & Data Collection

- Custom data forms with paid form support
- Form responses management and templates

### Media & Integrations

- Video streaming (100ms integration)
- Payment processing (Stripe)
- Google Maps, Sanity CMS, Sentry
- Push notifications and QR codes

## Target Users

1. **Community Organizers**: Managing events and members
2. **Event Attendees**: Participating in events and networking
3. **Hackathon Participants**: Teams joining hackathons
4. **Content Creators**: Sharing knowledge and projects
5. **Sponsors**: Supporting community events

## Technical Highlights

- **Angular 19.2.9** with Server-Side Rendering (SSR)
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)** with offline capabilities
- **Nx monorepo** for modular development
- **Tailwind CSS** with mobile-first responsive design
- **WebSocket** integration for real-time features
- Deployed on **AWS Elastic Beanstalk**
